Types of Smartphones

Smartphones are designed for different users and purposes. Below are the common types available today.

🎮 Gaming Smartphones

Built for high-performance gaming with powerful processors, advanced cooling systems, high refresh-rate displays, and large batteries.

📷 Camera Smartphones

Designed for photography enthusiasts with high-resolution cameras, optical zoom, image stabilization, and AI features.

💼 Business Smartphones

Focus on productivity, security, long battery life, and professional applications for business users.

💰 Budget Smartphones

Affordable devices that provide essential smartphone features at a lower price.

⭐ Flagship Smartphones

Premium devices with the latest processors, cameras, displays, and advanced technologies.

📱 Foldable Smartphones

Innovative smartphones with flexible screens that can fold into compact sizes.

🛡️ Rugged Smartphones

Built to withstand water, dust, extreme temperatures, and accidental drops, making them ideal for outdoor work.

📚 Student Smartphones

Affordable devices suitable for online learning, web browsing, video calls, and educational applications.
